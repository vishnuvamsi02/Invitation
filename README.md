# Ravi & Sowjanya — Wedding Invitation

A royal, mobile-first, fully responsive digital wedding invitation with interactive ceremonies guide, live countdown timer, photo gallery, RSVP via WhatsApp, and celebratory animations.

## ✨ Features

- **Royal Entry Screen:**
  - Lord Ganesha blessing and custom *BPVL* emblem (*"Two Souls, One Light"*).
  - Ambient breathing mandala bloom and floating flower petals.
  - Interactive "Open Invitation" button triggering a GSAP zoom transition and celebratory confetti burst.
- **Ambient Audio Controller:**
  - Floating music toggle (*"Sound On / Sound Off"*) with gentle autoplay on invitation opening.
- **Hero & Couple Details:**
  - Muhurat badge chip (*Thursday, 27th August 2026 • 11:40 PM*).
  - Royal gold arch portrait cards for the Groom (*Ravi Sri Surya Mani Shankar*) & Bride (*Sowjanya*) with family blessings.
- **Celebration Schedule:**
  - 5 ritual cards with custom circular icons (*Pelli Koduku*, *Pelli Kuturu*, *Haldi*, *Wedding Ceremony*, *Reception*).
  - Haldi ceremony dress code badge (*DRESS CODE: YELLOW*).
  - Direct Google Maps navigation links for each venue.
- **Live Countdown Timer:**
  - Real-time countdown ticking down days, hours, minutes, and seconds to the Muhurat.
- **"Memories in Bloom" Photo Gallery:**
  - 10 high-resolution wedding photos with click-to-zoom interactive lightbox modal and arrow key navigation.
- **Venue Guide & Travel:**
  - 5 event pill chips, venue details, embedded interactive Google Map, and travel notes.
- **RSVP & Share:**
  - Interactive *Joyfully Accept* vs *Regretfully Decline* toggle.
  - Event attendance checkboxes and adult/children guest counters.
  - Generates pre-filled WhatsApp RSVP message.
- **Spread The Joy:**
  - WhatsApp share button.
  - One-click `.ics` calendar file download (*Add to Calendar*).
  - Copy link button with clipboard feedback.

## 📱 Mobile-First Responsive Design

Crafted and tested to render fluidly across all devices, from small smartphones (360px) to tablets and high-resolution desktop displays.

1. Navigate to repository **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment** &rarr; **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`.
3. Click **Save**.
